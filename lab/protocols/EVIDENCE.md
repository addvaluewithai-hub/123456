# Evidence Protocol

## Evidence states

Always label important claims as one of:

1. analytical/scaling result;
2. our numerical simulation;
3. published simulation;
4. published experiment;
5. our bench measurement;
6. independent replication.

Never collapse these categories.

## Source priority

Prefer primary papers, patents for disclosed mechanisms, standards, official manufacturer/material data, theses with methods/data, and independent experiments. Use secondary summaries for discovery/context, not as the sole support for decisive technical claims.

For every material source capture URL/DOI, publication, date when available, what exact claim it supports, and confidence/limitations. A paper proving one device failed or succeeded does not automatically decide an entire architecture family.

## Anomalies

Non-peer-reviewed or surprising anomaly claims are leads, not proof. Reproduction requires independent accounting/methods. For FEA anomalies use at least two force/energy methods where possible plus mesh/path convergence and equality of initial/final material/field state.
