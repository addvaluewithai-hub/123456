# Decision R081 — compact EVOSM downgrade

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-08-22
Branch: EVOSM-01
Verdict: LOW PRIORITY for 10 W / ≤2 L compact architecture

Decision: stop active compact EVOSM optimization. The measured 1000 cm² output density (0.66 W/m²) requires 15.15 m² for 10 W. With the reported ~100 µm membrane thickness, membrane solid alone occupies ~1.515 L; the absolute 2 L stack pitch is ~132 µm. Engineering pitches of 0.5–1.0 mm require ~3.8–7.6× higher large-area power density. This is sufficient to remove the compact branch from active desk work without claiming a fundamental thermodynamic impossibility.

Preserve: distributed-area evaporation-maintained osmotic generation as a data-watch niche. Reopen only on matched large-area regeneration mass balance, crossover/retention, auxiliary-power data, or materially thinner membranes/modules retaining large-area power density.
