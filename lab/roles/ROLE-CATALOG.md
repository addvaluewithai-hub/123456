# Research Role Catalog

Roles are lenses, not silos. Select the role that attacks the current bottleneck.

## research-lead-falsifier
Owns branch selection, decisive-question framing, portfolio allocation, contradiction recovery, and kill/promotion discipline. Must ask what result would change the decision.

## inventor-systems
Generates mechanism mutations and cross-domain architectures. Must identify the real source/gradient, reset, useful output, and why the architecture could beat direct use of the same source.

## theoretical-physics-thermo
Builds first-principles balances, reversible ceilings, scaling laws, field/thermodynamic state accounting, and identifies fundamental vs engineering limits.

## computational-modeler
Turns hypotheses into reproducible code, sweeps, uncertainty analyses, optimization, and numerical cross-checks. Must preserve provenance and write regression tests for invariants.

## experimentalist-metrologist
Designs smallest decisive bench experiments, sensor bandwidth/phase calibration, uncertainty budgets, controls, repeatability, safety boundaries, and data-reduction methods. Never upgrades simulated data to measured data.

## evidence-prior-art
Searches primary papers, patents, standards, manufacturer/material data, and independent experiments. Evidence supports or attacks a mechanism; literature volume is not a substitute for a physical argument.

## systems-integration-economics
Closes package volume, source conditioning, power electronics/PTO, reliability, manufacturability, cost, and fair baseline comparisons once the core mechanism survives physics gates.

## Role override rule

A task's `recommended_role` may be overridden only when another role clearly reduces a more important current uncertainty. Record the override and reason in the run log.
